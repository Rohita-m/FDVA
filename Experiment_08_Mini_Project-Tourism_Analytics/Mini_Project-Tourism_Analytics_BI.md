# Mini Project - Tourism Analytics (PowerBI & Tableau)

## PowerBI Instructions
1. Load `tourism_data.csv` into PowerBI Desktop.
2. **Sum of Revenue, Sum of Visitors and Sum of Rating by Country:** Create a Stacked Bar Chart with `Country` on the Y-axis and `Revenue`, `Visitors`, and `Rating` on the X-axis.
3. **Sum of Revenue by Accommodation_Available and Country:** Create a Clustered Column Chart with `Accommodation_Available` on the X-axis, `Country` as the Legend, and `Revenue` on the Y-axis.
4. **Sum of Visitors, Sum of Revenue... by Category:** Create a Scatter Plot mapping `Visitors` (X-axis) vs `Revenue` (Y-axis), categorized by `Category` (Legend).
5. **Sum of Visitors, Sum of Revenue and Sum of Rating by Category:** Create a Donut Chart showing the distribution of these metrics broken down by `Category`.

## Tableau Instructions (Smart Tourism Prediction & Analytics Dashboard)
1. Connect Tableau to the generated `festival_data.csv` and `cost_data.csv`.
2. **Pie Chart of Seasons:** Create a Pie Chart using the `Season` field from festival data to show proportional distribution.
3. **Count of Distinct Festival Per Country:** Create a Filled Map visualization using the `Country` geographical role and counting distinct festivals.
4. **Line Chart:** Create a line chart plotting metrics across countries or time to detect trends.
5. **Affordability Index Per Country:** Create a horizontal Bar Chart showing the `Affordability Index` across different countries, sorted descending.
6. Assemble these sheets into the **Smart Tourism Prediction & Analytics Dashboard**.
